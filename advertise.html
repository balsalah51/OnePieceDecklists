Ad layout

The AdSense script is in the page head and loads asynchronously. The site does not reserve empty boxes for ads, and ads are not drawn as navigation items. Auto ads can still insert units after load and move content. If that shows up on a phone, turn off anchor and vignette formats in AdSense rather than adding a fake slot.

Consent

A Google-certified consent message is not turned on in the repo. Configure it in AdSense under Privacy & messaging, as described on the privacy page.
