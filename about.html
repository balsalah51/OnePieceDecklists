About

One Piece Decklists publishes OPTCG decklists, a tier list, leader pages, and guides for the Bandai ONE PIECE CARD GAME.

Tournament lists are sourced from Limitless Play and labeled on each page. Community lists are labeled with their public source. This is a fan project.

Questions and deck corrections go through the contact page. Advertising and partnership notes are on the media kit.

Fan site for the Bandai ONE PIECE CARD GAME (OPTCG). Not affiliated with Bandai or Shueisha.
