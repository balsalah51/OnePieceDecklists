EB05 spoilers

EXTRA BOOSTER -ONE PIECE HEROINES EDITION vol.2- [EB-05] is the second Heroines extra booster. English release is 30 October 2026. The numbered list is EB05-001 through EB05-061. This page also shows the six reprint Leaders and all eight SP cards, 75 card types. The two DON!! cards are still unpublished on Bandai's English list as of 25 October 2026. Numbered wording follows that list. EB05 lists collects complete public decks that play these cards, including the new green/yellow Nico Robin leader.

EB05 FAQ

Release facts
When does EB05 release?

English EB-05 Extra Booster ONE PIECE HEROINES EDITION vol.2 releases 30 October 2026 at $4.99 a pack. Japan is 31 October 2026. Cards become tournament legal 7 days after the release date in your region, and from the pre-release date at your store.

What is in EB05?

EB-05 is EXTRA BOOSTER -ONE PIECE HEROINES EDITION vol.2-. Bandai lists 75 card types plus 2 DON!! cards: 7 Leaders, 29 Commons, 21 Rares, 9 Super Rares, 1 Secret Rare, and 8 Special cards. The theme is heroine characters. Nico Robin debuts as a Leader.

Which EB05 leaders are in the set?

Seven Leaders: new Nico Robin (EB05-010, green/yellow), plus reprints with new art of Shirahoshi (OP11-022), Nami (OP11-041), Jewelry Bonney (OP13-100), Boa Hancock (OP14-041), Rebecca (OP15-039), and Nefeltari Vivi (EB03-001).

Is the full EB05 card list out?

The official numbered list is EB05-001 through EB05-061 (61 cards). This page also lists the six reprint Leaders and eight SP cards, 75 card types in all. Bandai's English list, still current on 25 October 2026, does not publish the two DON!! cards, so their art and text are not shown. Numbered-card wording follows that official English list. Reprint and SP effects are the original cards. The purple 1-cost Nico Robin is EB05-033.
