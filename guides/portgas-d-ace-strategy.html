Portgas D. Ace strategy

Red Portgas D. Ace (OP16-001) is the OP17 Rush Leader, not the red/blue OP13 Ace. Once per turn he gives Rush to a Monkey D. Luffy Character or to a Whitebeard Pirates Character with 8000 power or more. The deck does not grind like Sabo or Rocks. It sequences searchers into Rush bodies and punches life every turn those bodies have Rush.

Limitless pairings in the OP17 window have Ace at 57.8% versus Mihawk in 45 games. New Realm’s 1 September report called him the deck that punishes slow lists — not a Tier 1 next to Mihawk / Rocks / Sabo. This page is the curve for the 67 OP17 files inside the 143-list hub.

How the leader works

OP16-001 · 5 life · 5000 · Special

The activate is Main, once per turn. Legal Rush targets: any [Monkey.D.Luffy] Character, or a Whitebeard Pirates Character that is already 8000 or more. Marco (OP16-014, 6 cost / 8000), Curiel (OP16-004, 7 cost / 8000), Kingdew (OP17-006, 7 cost / 8000), OP16 Newgate (OP16-003, 8 cost / 10000), and OP17 Newgate (OP17-005, 10 cost / 12000) all qualify. Character Ace (OP16-118) is 6000 and does not. Little Oars Jr. is Whitebeard Pirates Allies, not Whitebeard Pirates.

4-cost Luffy (OP16-015) is a Luffy, so he can take Rush. His other line is on the opponent’s attack: trash an 8000-power Character from hand and Ace plus Luffy become 7000 for the turn. That is a defense trick, not a standing pump.

The current 50-card core

Averaged from 67 OP17 lists on this site

Eight cards are 4-ofs: Monkey D. Garp (OP13-016), Little Oars Jr. (OP16-017), Marco (OP16-014), Curiel (OP16-004), Kingdew (OP17-006), Edward Newgate (OP16-003 and OP17-005), and Moby Dick (OP16-021). The 3-ofs are Namule (OP16-010), Monkey D. Luffy (OP16-015), Portgas D. Ace (OP16-118), Vista (OP16-011), and If You’re Coming with Me Kiss Your Lives Goodbye!! (OP16-020). Uta (ST23-001) is the 2-of. Ga Ha Ha Ha!! (OP17-017) is the 1-of.

Two Newgates are not a typo: OP16-003 is the 8-cost, OP17-005 is the 10-cost. OPDeckGuide’s Ace writeup is the public curve this page follows.

The curve

Go second · public OP17 writeup + hosted lists

Going second is the seat. The Rush activate wants a body that costs what you just spent, then an attack the same turn.

DON!! 1–2. Garp is a 1-cost searcher: look at 4, take a cost-3-or-more. Moby Dick is a 1-cost Stage that looks at 3 and later trashes itself to attach a rested DON!!. Missing both is why you mulligan. Namule is the other 1-cost if you already have an 8000 in hand to reveal.

DON!! 4. Luffy, or Little Oars Jr. as the 4-of 8000 Blocker. Luffy is the 3-of you keep Garp to find. He does not pump the Leader on your turn.

DON!! 5–6. Character Ace searches a Luffy or Whitebeard Pirates card. Marco is the 4-of 6-cost: give him Rush and swing. Vista is the 3-of 6-cost 8000 that also takes Rush and draws if you reveal an 8000. Uta is a 6-cost Blocker that costs 2 if you already have a 10000-power Character — she is not a searcher.

DON!! 7–8. Curiel or Kingdew if you need an 8000 Rush body on 7. OP16 Newgate on 8: your Leader gets Double Attack and +2000, and you can −6000 an opposing Character if you reveal two 8000s. Activate Ace, swing the 8-cost. This is the first real life-pressure turn.

DON!! 10. OP17 Newgate costs 10, or 6 if the opponent already has a 10000-power Character, and makes your Leader 8000 until the end of their next turn. Character Ace is a closer and a searcher, not the 1-cost you keep instead of Garp.

Going first, keep Garp plus a 4-cost and accept that the first Rush Newgate is a turn later. A hand of two 8-costs and Character Ace with no 1-drop is a mulligan on either seat.

Mulligan

What to keep · what to ship

OPDeckGuide’s keep is the one the consensus list is built for:

Keep: Garp + Moby Dick + 4-cost Luffy. That is the nut opener. Keep Garp + any 4-cost (Luffy or Little Oars Jr.) if Moby Dick is missing. Keep Moby Dick + Luffy if Garp is missing — you will be short a search, not a curve.

Ship: double Newgate, Character Ace + events, Uta with no 10000-power card to discount her, and Ga Ha Ha Ha!! as the only cheap card in an otherwise expensive hand. Uta is a 2-of Blocker, not the Garp replacement.

If you have Garp and no 4-cost, keep Garp and look at the rest of the hand: one Newgate is acceptable, two is not.

Tech cards

Flex from the 67-list OP17 average

Uta (2). 6-cost Blocker, 2-cost if a 10000-power Newgate is already in play. Do not cut Garp for a third Uta. Ga Ha Ha Ha!! (1). The 1-of +2000/−2000 Counter. A second copy is the first cut if you need room. If You’re Coming with Me… (3). Rest 1 DON!! and reveal an 8000 to draw 1, or +3000 Counter. Keep all three in the Mihawk and Sabo seats. Vista (3) / Namule (3). Vista is a 6-cost 8000 Rush body that draws; Namule is a 1-cost that K.O.s a 2000 if you reveal an 8000. Character Ace (3). 5-cost searcher. Trim to 2 if you are bricking 5-DON!! hands and the Newgates are already 8.

Win rates and matchups

Limitless pairings · then the public guide

Limitless constructed pairings from 28 August 2026, 16 events, 832 games, 8-game cutoff — same sample as the Mihawk pairing table:

Ace vs Mihawk — 57.8% · 45 games (26–19). Favored, smaller sample than Sabo or Rocks. Rush Newgate is how you race a Slash midrange list that wants to sit.

New Realm’s 1 September 2026 report lists Ace as the deck that punishes slow lists — not as a Tier 1 alongside Mihawk / Rocks / Sabo. A public Top 32 Utrecht Finals graphic counted 0 Ace next to 10 Mihawk, 7 Robin, and 6 Sabo. The pairing number versus Mihawk is real; the conversion in that Top 32 is not there yet.

OPDeckGuide’s qualitative strip, not a pairing table: favorable Mihawk, Sabo, Shanks. Unfavorable Nico Robin, Rocks, Linlin, Luffy & Ace, Enel, Boa. Play Ace when the room is Mihawk / Sabo / Shanks. Side or swap off when the room is Robin and Rocks — those are the lists that develop a board Ace cannot race after a missed 4-cost Luffy.
