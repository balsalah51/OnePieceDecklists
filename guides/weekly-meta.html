Weekly meta

Week of 16 October 2026. This note uses the same hosted lists and the same tier letters as the tier list (25 published sources, rolling 45 days, 1 September 2026 through 16 October 2026). It is not a copy of the Limitless or OPTCG.GG metagame page.

What the window is playing

1 September 2026 through 16 October 2026

The homepage leaders in that window are Mihawk (370, S), OP09 Nico Robin (286, A), Rocks D. Xebec (272, S). An older chart counted only the newest 200 lists, and a separate pie counted only lists that played an OP17 card. Mihawk lists often play no OP17 card, so that pie left him off a page that still called him S tier. Both views now count every hosted list in the rolling window.

Share of the 2681 lists in the window: Mihawk S 13.8% (370), OP09 Nico Robin A 10.7% (286), Rocks D. Xebec S 10.1% (272), Sabo S 8.5% (228), Luffy & Ace B 8.5% (228), Monkey D. Luffy A 7.5% (201), Kaido A 6.3% (168), OP15 Enel A 6.3% (168).

In the same window, Blackbeard has the best first-place rate among leaders with a full sample: 3 firsts from 15 placed lists (20.0%). That number is firsts divided by placed lists on this site. It is not a game win rate.

Where that sits on the tier list

Same letters as the board

OP09 Nico Robin and EB05 Nico Robin, OP15 Enel and OP05 Enel, and OP17 Shanks and OP09 Shanks are different cards. The labels on this page use the set code so they are not the same leader.

Large events are marked on the featured results and on recent lists. A regional or championship outranks a Rumble or Chinoize cup when both are top cuts. Online cups stay in the list. They just stop looking like the headline.
