Nico Robin strategy

Purple/Yellow Nico Robin (OP09-062) is a ramp deck that likes Trigger cards. She trashes a Trigger when she attacks, picks up extra DON!!, then spends it on yellow Big Mom bodies. If you enjoy a slow snowball that ends in a 10-cost Linlin, this is a really pleasant list to learn.

The Utrecht winner on this site uses the same shell: 10-cost Linlin, Sweet 3 Generals, Oven, Pudding, and Gum-Gum Giant. When the room is full of green Mihawk, Robin is the friendly answer. She won 47 of 80 decided games (58.8%) in the OP17 pairing sample.

How the leader works

OP09-062 · 4 life · 5000 · Strike
  • Robin is 4 life / 5000 with Strike and built-in Banish. Life she hits gets trashed, so the Trigger never fires.
  • On attack, trash one Trigger from hand, take a DON!! from the DON!! deck, and rest it. That is the whole engine.
  • You do not need a Stage. You just want a Trigger in hand so 6 DON!! becomes 7, or 7 becomes 8, and Linlin lands on time.
  • Opponents often Counter the 5000 leader swings they would rather eat. That empties their hand before your 10-cost turn, which is a nice bonus.
  • OPDeckGuide’s Robin writeup calls the same sequence rope-a-dope: flood 4k Trigger bodies, then Linlin turns those 4ks into 8ks. Use that page for the turn-by-turn DON!! count. The hosted consensus is the 50-card list people are actually registering.

The current 50-card core

Averaged from OP17 Robin lists on this site

The hub consensus is 89 OP17 lists filled to 50 cards. Think of it as a yellow Big Mom pile with a purple event glued on.

  • Play 4: Streusen (OP17-113), Charlotte Daifuku (OP17-107), Charlotte Pudding (OP17-109), Yamato (OP17-074), Charlotte Oven (OP17-102), Charlotte Smoothie (OP17-106), Sweet 3 Generals (OP17-114), Charlotte Linlin (OP17-112), and Gum-Gum Giant (OP09-078).
  • Play 3: Brulee, Borsalino, Perospero, and Marshall D. Teach.
  • Play 2: Baby 5, the usual flex slot.
  • Streusen and Pudding find the name. Oven recasts what Pudding trashed. Linlin is the turn you built the DON!! for. Giant is the 1-cost Counter that also pitches a brick.
  • Flame Flame Winner Utrecht (Benjo, 4 September 2026) keeps the same 4-ofs and shuffles the 3-ofs: 3 Teach, 3 Perospero, 3 OP05-073, 2 Brulee, and no Baby 5 / Borsalino. Start from the hub consensus, then peek at that Utrecht page if you want the event photo.

The curve

First or second · public OP17 writeup + hosted lists
  • Going first is the clean Linlin line. Search or draw on the low DON!! turns with Pudding or Streusen, attack with Robin so she adds a rested DON!!, then spend Oven (or It’s to Die For, if your list still has it) to keep the trash line live.
  • OPDeckGuide’s curve has Linlin on turn 4 if the Trigger discards hit. Hosted lists often cut It’s to Die For for more Giants and Teach. The DON!! still comes from the leader.
  • Going second, attach enough DON!! to swing 8000 with Robin, trash a Trigger, and keep the same 10-cost arrival one turn later.
  • If you miss Pudding or Streusen, you can end up with 10 DON!! and no Linlin. Keep a searcher plus a Trigger so that does not happen.

Why she beats Mihawk

Limitless pairings · 80 games

Green Mihawk recycles DON!! and rests the board. Robin does not need to win that rest war. She just presents 10-cost yellow threats and Banish life that do not care if a 5-cost got frozen.

  • On the Mihawk matchups page she is 47–33 (58.8%) in 80 games, the biggest reliable hole in that table.
  • OPDeckGuide also lists Mihawk as favorable for Robin, along with Ace and Shanks.
  • Rocks and Black Luffy are a little worse. Treat that as a curve note, not a reason to skip the pairing table.
  • If the room is hawks, this is a very comfortable list to register.

Matchups in short

Pairings first, then the public guide
  • Mihawk: play the 10-cost and the Banish attacks. Let them rest whatever they want.
  • Ace / Luffy & Ace: a race, not a bye. OPDeckGuide calls Ace favorable and Luffy & Ace a little worse. Keep Giant ready.
  • Sabo: even on the guide, and a common hawk pairing. Stay on the Linlin line.
  • Rocks: a little worse. Blue Rocks draws and clogs the board before you reach Linlin, so protect the Trigger in hand.
  • Kaido / Black Luffy: close. Keep Giant and a Trigger so the leader attack still ramps.
