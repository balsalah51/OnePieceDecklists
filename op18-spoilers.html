OP18 spoilers

BOOSTER PACK -THE DOMINANCE OF GOD- [OP-18] is the Water Seven and Holy Knights set. English release is 20 November 2026. Bandai published the numbered card list. This page shows those 119 cards with English names, stats, and effects. Pictures are official/sample art from Limitless.

20 Nov 2026English release
$4.99Pack MSRP
127+1Card types
119Numbered cards

Official product page: The Dominance of God [OP-18]. Saint Gunko and Saint Shamrock debut. Franky gets his first Leader. Yoshitaka Amano drew parallels of Saint Shamrock and of Loki, who returns as an OP17-119 reprint.

Loki stays the black OP17-119 Secret Rare already in the game: 6 cost, 8000 power, +12 cost, +3000 power on the opponent's turn, and on play it K.O.s opposing Characters with a total cost of 4 or less. The new part is the Amano artwork in this pack.

OP18 FAQ

Release facts
When does OP18 release?

English OP-18 The Dominance of God releases 20 November 2026 at $4.99 a pack. Japan is later in November 2026. Cards become tournament legal 7 days after the release date in your region, and from the pre-release date at your store.

What is in OP18?

OP-18 is BOOSTER PACK -THE DOMINANCE OF GOD-. Bandai lists 127 card types plus 1. The themes are Water Seven and the Holy Knights. Saint Gunko and Saint Shamrock debut, and Franky gets his first Leader card.

Which OP18 leaders are revealed?

Six leaders are revealed: Karoo (OP18-001, red/blue), Franky (OP18-021, green/purple), Monkey D. Luffy (OP18-022, green), Ms. All Sunday (OP18-041, blue), Saint Gunko (OP18-060, purple/black), and Spandam (OP18-079, black/yellow).

Is the full OP18 card list out?

Yes. Bandai posted the numbered OP18-001 to OP18-119 list. This page uses that public list. The product still lists 127+1 types because parallels and extras are separate. Sleeptids OP18-093 may be any number in a deck.
