Edward Newgate

Red Whitebeard Pirates package from OP-17. Trash a card on your opponent's attack to swing battles with a +4000 power boost.

Red Leader OP17-001 5 Life 5000 Power Special The Four Emperors/Whitebeard Pirates
[On Your Opponent's Attack] [Once Per Turn] You may trash 1 card from your hand: Up to 1 of your Leader or Characters gains +4000 power during this battle.

How it plays

Staples from lists on this page

Red OP17 Edward Newgate is a Whitebeard beatstick that keeps 8000-power bodies on the board. Core lists play 4 Sanji, 4 Portgas D. Ace, 4 Izo, 4 ten-cost Edward Newgate, 4 Kouzuki Oden, plus Marco, Uta, and Moby Dick. Flex is Namule/Curiel search, Rakuyo beatdown, or an Ivankov/Zoro package.
