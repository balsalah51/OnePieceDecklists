Charlotte Linlin

Yellow Big Mom Pirates package from OP-17. Attack, trash a card, and force a Life or hand tax.

Yellow Leader OP17-099 5 Life 5000 Power Special The Four Emperors/Big Mom Pirates
[When Attacking] You may trash 1 card from your hand: Your opponent chooses one: • Trash 1 card from your hand. Then, add up to 1 card from the top of your deck to the top of your Life cards. • Trash 1 card from your opponent's hand.

How it plays

Staples from lists on this page

Yellow OP17 Charlotte Linlin is a Big Mom swarm leader. Pudding, the on-color Linlin, and Cracker are in every averaged list; Katakuri, Oven, Sweet 3 Generals, Daifuku, and Zeus are close behind. Perospero, Smoothie, Streusen, Teach, and Brulee fill the rest.

Consensus list

Averaged from 36 lists on this page, using the 36 that play OP17 so the 50-card core includes the new set, then filled to 50 cards.
