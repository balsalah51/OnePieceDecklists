Rocks D. Xebec

Blue Rocks Pirates package from OP-17. Attack, trash a card, and dig for crew to draw extra cards.

Blue Leader OP17-039 5 Life 5000 Power Slash Rocks Pirates
[When Attacking] You may trash 1 card from your hand: Reveal 1 card from the top of your deck. If the revealed card's type includes "Rocks Pirates", draw 2 cards.

How it plays

Staples from lists on this page

Blue OP17 Rocks D. Xebec is the densest Limitless pile on this site. Lists play 4 Edward Newgate, 4 Shiki, 4 Charlotte Linlin, 4 Gloriosa, 4 Miss Buckingham Stussy, 4 Rocks D. Xebec, the Rocks Pirates stage, and There's No Authority. Kaido, Streusen, Don Marlon, and Captain John are the usual ratio fights.
