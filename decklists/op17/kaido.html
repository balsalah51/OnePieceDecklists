Kaido

Purple Animal Kingdom Pirates package from OP-17. Spend DON!! during attacks to shrink opposing Characters.

Purple Leader OP17-058 5 Life 5000 Power Special The Four Emperors/Animal Kingdom Pirates
[When Attacking]/[On Your Opponent's Attack] [Once Per Turn] DON!! −1: Give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −2000 power during this turn.

How it plays

Staples from lists on this page

Purple OP17 Kaido is All-Star midrange with a tight tournament core. King, Queen, Basil Hawkins, Yamato, the on-leader Kaido, Mamaragan, and We're Going to Claim the One Piece show up in every averaged list. Flex is Black Maria, Jack, Divine Departure, or the new OP17 events.
