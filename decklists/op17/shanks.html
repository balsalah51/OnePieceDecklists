Shanks

Green Red-Haired Pirates package from OP-17. Rest opposing Characters and keep them from standing on the next refresh.

Green Leader OP17-020 5 Life 5000 Power Slash The Four Emperors/Red-Haired Pirates
[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] You may trash 1 card from your hand or rest 1 of your DON!! cards: Up to 1 of your opponent's rested Characters will not become active in your opponent's next Refresh Phase.

How it plays

Staples from lists on this page

Green OP17 Shanks rests the board and plays Red Hair Pirates. Every list locks Benn Beckman, Yasopp, and the ten-cost Shanks; most also play Limejuice, Lucky Roux, Crone Oli, and rest events. The split is staying in-theme versus splashing Perona, Smoker, and Law like Mihawk.

Consensus list

Averaged from 14 lists on this page, using the 14 that play OP17 so the 50-card core includes the new set, then filled to 50 cards.
