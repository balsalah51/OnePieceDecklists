Crocodile

Black OP14 Crocodile. Baroque Works. Opponent characters cannot be removed by your effects. K.O. a Baroque Works character to give an opposing character −10 cost.

Black LeaderOP14-0795 Life5000 PowerSpecialThe Seven Warlords of the Sea/Baroque Works
All of your opponent's Characters cannot be removed from the field by your effects. [Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] You may K.O. 1 of your Characters with a type including "Baroque Works": Give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −10 cost during this turn. Then, you may trash 2 cards from the top of your deck.
