Sabo · ST13-001

How it plays

Staples from lists on this page

ST13 Sabo is red/yellow Three Brothers Sabo - not red/black OP13 Sabo. OP17 yellow Big Mom cards are the current splash.

Consensus list

Averaged from 6 lists on this page, using the 3 that play OP17 so the 50-card core includes the new set, then filled to 50 cards.
