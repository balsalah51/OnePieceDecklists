ST13 Luffy

Black/Yellow LeaderST13-0034 Life5000 PowerStrikeSupernovas/Straw Hat Crew
Your face-up Life cards are placed at the bottom of your deck instead of being added to your hand, according to the rules. [DON!! x2] [Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] You may trash 1 card from your hand: If you have 0 Life cards, add up to 2 Character cards with a cost of 5 from your hand or trash to the top of your Life cards face-up.

Monkey D. Luffy · ST13-003

How it plays

Staples from lists on this page

ST13 Luffy is black/yellow Three Brothers Luffy. Hosted lists splash OP17 Luffy as a finisher.

Consensus list

Averaged from 2 lists on this page, then folding in OP17 cards from the 2 current-format lists, then filled to 50 cards.
