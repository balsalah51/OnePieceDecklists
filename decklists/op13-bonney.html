OP13 Bonney

Yellow OP13 Jewelry Bonney is the Trigger-ramp Bonney leader. Not red/yellow EB04 Bonney. Hosted lists splash OP17 yellow cards.

How it plays

Staples from lists on this page

Yellow OP13 Jewelry Bonney ramps DON!! when you play a Trigger character. OP17 yellow Big Mom cards are the current splash. Not red/yellow EB04 Bonney and not green OP07 Bonney.
