OP09 Shanks

Red OP09-001 Shanks. Not green OP17-020 Shanks. OP17 Shanks (OP17-020) is a different leader.

OP09 Shanks constructed lists from Limitless events.

[Once Per Turn] This effect can be activated when your opponent attacks. Give up to 1 of your opponent's Leader or Character cards −1000 power during this turn.

Shanks · OP09-001

How it plays

Staples from lists on this page

Red OP09 Shanks is the older Red Hair leader - not green OP17 Shanks. Hosted lists keep the Red Hair pile and splash an OP17 Newgate body.

Consensus list

Averaged from 4 lists on this page, using the 3 that play OP17 so the 50-card core includes the new set, then filled to 50 cards.
