Koby

Red/Black Koby. Navy / SWORD. SWORD characters can attack the turn they are played. A 7000-power-or-less Navy body can be saved from removal.

Red/Black LeaderOP11-0014 Life5000 PowerStrikeNavy/SWORD
Your {SWORD} type Characters can attack Characters on the turn in which they are played. [Once Per Turn] If your {Navy} type Character with 7000 base power or less would be removed from the field by your opponent's effect, you may place 3 cards from your trash at the bottom of your deck in any order instead.

Koby · OP11-001

How it plays

Staples from lists on this page

Red/Black Koby is Navy / SWORD. SWORD characters can attack the turn they come down, and a 7000-or-less Navy body can be saved from removal. Averaged lists lock Kujyaku, Aramaki, Helmeppo, Ripper, and I'm Gonna Be a Navy Officer; Doll, both Koby bodies, Prince Grus, and Hibari are the next cuts.
