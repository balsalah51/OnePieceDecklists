Yamato

Black OP16 Yamato. Land of Wano. A Wano character played from trash gains Rush that turn. Not a Kaido character card.

Black LeaderOP16-0795 Life5000 PowerStrikeLand of Wano
When a {Land of Wano} type Character card is played from your trash, that Character gains [Rush] during this turn. (This card can attack on the turn in which it is played.)

Yamato · OP16-079

How it plays

Staples from lists on this page

Black OP16 Yamato is the Wano leader, not a character in Kaido. A Land of Wano character played from trash gains Rush that turn. Averaged lists lock Nami, the Yamato bodies, both Momonosuke, Kin'emon, Shinobu, Nico Robin, and I've Come Here To Cut Those Chains; Otama and Ground Death are close.

Consensus list

Averaged from 103 lists on this page, using the 9 that play OP17 so the 50-card core includes the new set, then filled to 50 cards.
