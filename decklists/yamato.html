Yamato

Yamato · OP16-079

How it plays

Staples from lists on this page

Black OP16 Yamato is the Wano leader, not a character in Kaido. A Land of Wano character played from trash gains Rush that turn. Averaged lists lock Nami, the Yamato bodies, both Momonosuke, Kin'emon, Shinobu, Nico Robin, and I've Come Here To Cut Those Chains; Otama and Ground Death are close.
