Sabo · OP13-004

How it plays

Staples from lists on this page

Red/Black OP13 Sabo is Revolutionary Army midrange, not ST13 Sabo. Current-format lists splash OP17 Elbaph Straw Hats and Loki to turn the Sabo engine on.
