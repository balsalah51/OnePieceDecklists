Nico Robin

Green/Yellow EB05 Nico Robin. Straw Hat Crew, Wisdom. Once per turn, a non-Blocker Wisdom character being K.O.'d adds a Life, and she can set one Wisdom character with 6000 power or less as active. Not purple/yellow OP09-062 Robin.

Green/Yellow LeaderEB05-0104 Life5000 PowerWisdomStraw Hat Crew
[Once Per Turn] When one of your Characters with the Wisdom attribute and without [Blocker] is K.O.'d, add up to 1 card from the top of your deck to the top of your Life cards. [Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] Set up to 1 of your Characters with the Wisdom attribute and 6000 power or less as active.
