Imu

Black OP13 Imu. You cannot play 2-cost or higher events. At the start of the game you may play a Mary Geoise stage, then trash Celestial Dragons or a card from hand to draw.

Black Leader OP13-079 4 Life 5000 Power
Under the rules of this game, you cannot include Events with a cost of 2 or more in your deck and at the start of the game, play up to 1 {Mary Geoise} type Stage card from your deck. [Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] You may trash 1 of your {Celestial Dragons} type Characters or 1 card from your hand: Draw 1 card.

Black OP13 Imu. Not OP17 Elbaph Luffy.

How it plays

Staples from lists on this page

Black OP13 Imu is Mary Geoise / Five Elders, not OP17 Elbaph Luffy. No 2-cost or higher events, and the Empty Throne stage starts in play from deck. Lists play Saturn, Warcury, Nusjuro, Mars, Ju Peter, the 10-cost Five Elders, Saint Shalria, and The Five Elders Are at Your Service. Saint Charlos, Sabo, Never Existed, and Ground Death are flex.
