OP05 Enel

OP05 Enel constructed lists from Limitless events.

Yellow LeaderOP05-0984 Life5000 PowerSpecialSky Island
[Opponent's Turn] [Once Per Turn] When your number of Life cards becomes 0, add 1 card from the top of your deck to the top of your Life cards. Then, trash 1 card from your hand.

Enel · OP05-098

How it plays

Staples from lists on this page

Yellow OP05 Enel is the 4-life yellow Enel - not purple OP15 Enel. Current lists splash OP17 yellow Big Mom cards.

Consensus list

Averaged from 51 lists on this page, using the 51 that play OP17 so the 50-card core includes the new set, then filled to 50 cards.
