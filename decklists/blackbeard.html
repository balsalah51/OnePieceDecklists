Blackbeard

Black/Yellow OP16 Marshall D. Teach. Blackbeard Pirates. Your characters cost +1 on the opponent's turn; trash a Trigger to retarget an attack.

Black/Yellow LeaderOP16-0804 Life5000 PowerSpecialThe Seven Warlords of the Sea/Blackbeard Pirates
[Opponent's Turn] All of your Characters gain +1 cost. [On Your Opponent's Attack] [Once Per Turn] You may trash 1 card with a [Trigger] from your hand: Change the target of that attack to this Leader or to one of your {Blackbeard Pirates} type Character cards.

Marshall D. Teach · OP16-080

How it plays

Staples from lists on this page

Black/Yellow OP16 Marshall D. Teach is Blackbeard, not a different Teach printing. Your characters cost +1 on the opponent's turn; trash a Trigger to retarget an attack. Averaged lists lock Shiryu, both Teach bodies, Catarina Devon, Vasco Shot, and Zehahahahaha; Borsalino, Doc Q, Fullalead, My Era Begins, Van Augur, and Burgess follow.

Consensus list

Averaged from 126 lists on this page, then folding in OP17 cards from the 2 current-format lists, then filled to 50 cards.
