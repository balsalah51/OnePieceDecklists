How it plays

Staples from lists on this page

Blue OP16 Buggy is the Impel Down / Buggy Pirates leader - not a character in GB Luffy. When an Impel Down body leaves the field, DON!! ×1 plays a Prisoner of Impel Down from hand. Averaged lists lock Prisoner of Impel Down, Mr. 1, Mr. 2, Mr. 3, Buggy, and Crocodile; Slave Arrow and Miss Olive are the next cuts.
